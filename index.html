How to Play

Blackjack

This is a simplified version of the classic Blackjack game.

  • At the beginning of the game, both the player and the opponent draw two cards from a deck of 52 cards.
  • The opponent reveals only one of their cards, while the other remains hidden.
  • The player must decide whether to Hit (draw another card) or Stand (keep their current hand) based on their total points.
  • Once the player stands, it becomes the opponent's turn.
  • The player who exceeds 21 points loses immediately.
  • A total of exactly 21 wins the game.
  • If neither player exceeds 21, the player with the higher total wins.
  • If both players have the same total, the game ends in a draw.
  • In this version, an Ace is worth 11 points, while Jacks, Queens, and Kings are each worth 10 points.

Cat and Mouse

Cat and Mouse is a fun clicking game.

  • The mouse runs randomly around the screen on its own.
  • When you click on the mouse, you catch it and earn a point.
  • Right after you catch one mouse, a brand-new mouse will appear and start running around again.

Try to get the highest score you can!
